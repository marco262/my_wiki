Character class provides a character's most exciting capabilities. This chapter offers twelve classes, each of which contains four subclasses -- all summarized below.

## [[[class:Barbarian]]]

Storm with Rage, and wade into hand-to-hand combat. Then follow the...

 - [[[subclass:Path of the Berserker]]] to unleash raw violence.
 - [[[subclass:Path of the Wild Heart]]] to manifest kinship with animals.
 - [[[subclass:Path of the World Tree]]] to tap into cosmic vitality.
 - [[[subclass:Path of the Zealot]]] to rage in union with a god.

## [[[class:Bard]]]

Perform spells that inspire and heal allies or beguile foes. Then join the...

 - [[[subclass:College of Dance]]] to harness agility in battle.
 - [[[subclass:College of Glamour]]] to weave beguiling Feywild magic.
 - [[[subclass:College of Lore]]] to collect knowledge and magical secrets.
 - [[[subclass:College of Valor]]] to wield weapons with spells.

## [[[class:Cleric]]]

Invoke divine magic to heal, bolster, and smite. Then harness the...

 - [[[subclass:Life Domain]]] to be a master of healing.
 - [[[subclass:Light Domain]]] to wield searing, warding light.
 - [[[subclass:Trickery Domain]]] to bedevil foes with mischief.
 - [[[subclass:War Domain]]] to inspire valor and chastise foes.

## [[[class:Druid]]]

Channel nature magic to heal, shape-shift, and control the elements. Then join the...

 - [[[subclass:Circle of the Land]]] to draw on the magic of the environment.
 - [[[subclass:Circle of the Moon]]] to adopt powerful animal forms.
 - [[[subclass:Circle of the Sea]]] to channel tides and storms.
 - [[[subclass:Circle of the Stars]]] to gain powers in a starry form.

## [[[class:Fighter]]]

Master all weapons and armor. Then embody the...

 - [[[subclass:Battle Master]]] to use special combat maneuvers.
 - [[[subclass:Champion]]] to strive for peak combat prowess.
 - [[[subclass:Eldritch Knight]]] to learn spells to aid in combat.
 - [[[subclass:Psi Warrior]]] to augment attacks with psionic power.

## [[[class:Monk]]]

Dart in and out of melee while striking fast and hard. Then become a...

 - [[[subclass:Warrior of Mercy]]] to heal or harm with a touch.
 - [[[subclass:Warrior of Shadow]]] to employ shadows for subterfuge.
 - [[[subclass:Warrior of the Elements]]] to wield elemental power.
 - [[[subclass:Warrior of the Open Hand]]] to master unarmed combat.

## [[[class:Paladin]]]

Smite foes and shield allies with divine and martial might. Then swear the...

 - [[[subclass:Oath of Devotion]]] to emulate the angels of justice.
 - [[[subclass:Oath of Glory]]] to reach the heights of heroism.
 - [[[subclass:Oath of the Ancients]]] to preserve life, joy, and nature.
 - [[[subclass:Oath of Vengeance]]] to hunt down evildoers.

## [[[class:Ranger]]]

Weave together martial prowess, nature magic, and survival skills. Then embody the...

 - [[[subclass:Beast Master]]] to bond with a primal beast.
 - [[[subclass:Fey Wanderer]]] to manifest fey mirth and fury.
 - [[[subclass:Gloom Stalker]]] to hunt foes that lurk in darkness.
 - [[[subclass:Hunter]]] to protect nature with martial versatility.

## [[[class:Rogue]]]

Launch deadly Sneak Attacks while avoiding harm through stealth. Then embody the...

 - [[[subclass:Arcane Trickster]]] to enhance stealth with spells.
 - [[[subclass:Assassin]]] to deliver ambushes and poison.
 - [[[subclass:Soulknife]]] to strike foes with psi blades.
 - [[[subclass:Thief]]] to master infiltration and treasure hunting.

## [[[class:Sorcerer]]]

Wield magic innate to your being, shaping the power to your will. Then channel...

 - [[[subclass:Aberrant Sorcery]]] to use strange psionic magic.
 - [[[subclass:Clockwork Sorcery]]] to harness cosmic forces of order.
 - [[[subclass:Draconic Sorcery]]] to breathe the magic of dragons.
 - [[[subclass:Wild Magic Sorcery]]] to unleash chaos magic.

## [[[class:Warlock]]]

Cast spells derived from occult knowledge. Then form a pact with the...

 - [[[subclass:Archfey Patron]]] to teleport and wield fey magic.
 - [[[subclass:Celestial Patron]]] to heal with heavenly magic.
 - [[[subclass:Fiend Patron]]] to call on sinister powers.
 - [[[subclass:Great  Old  One Patron]]] to delve into forbidden lore.

## [[[class:Wizard]]]

Study arcane magic and master spells for every purpose. Then embody the...

 - [[[subclass:Abjurer]]] to shield allies and banish foes.
 - [[[subclass:Diviner]]] to learn the multiverse's secrets.
 - [[[subclass:Evoker]]] to create explosive effects.
 - [[[subclass:Illusionist]]] to weave spells of deception.
