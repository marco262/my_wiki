# A Deep and Creeping Darkness

A field investigation into a mountain village that vanished after a mining disaster. Your class is sent to research old records, speak with the last people who remember the place, and find out whether the abandoned settlement is truly empty.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** investigation mystery, spooky field trip, dungeon crawl

# Shemshime's Bedtime Rhyme

Your class takes a field trip to Candlekeep, the legendary fortress-library, for a rare tour of its archives and restoration rooms. But when a strange children's rhyme begins spreading through the lower stacks, the visit is cut short by an emergency quarantine. Trapped inside with Avowed librarians, visiting scholars, and your classmates, you'll need to uncover the source of the curse before the song takes hold.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** locked-room mystery, quarantine, creepy curse

# The Sunless Citadel

Your class is sent to investigate a ruined fortress that sank into the earth long ago. The nearby village knows the place for two strange things: a magical fruit with miraculous healing properties, and the people who disappear when they go looking for its source. Inside the buried citadel, you'll find old traps, rival dungeon factions, and signs that something unnatural is spreading beneath the surface.

**Length:** Medium (2 levels)

**Intended Level Range:** Levels 1-3

**Tags:** dungeon crawl, lost ruin, corrupted nature, faction choices

# The Forge of Fury

Your class follows rumors of Khundrukar, a lost dwarven stronghold hidden beneath a mountain called the Stone Tooth. Centuries ago, the fortress belonged to a legendary smith whose weapons are still spoken of with awe, but the halls have since fallen to raiders, cave monsters, and darker things below. This is a dangerous expedition into a layered underground ruin, with the chance to uncover lost craft, old history, and treasure that still has teeth.

**Length:** Medium (2 levels)

**Intended Level Range:** Levels 3-5

**Tags:** dungeon crawl, lost stronghold, dwarven ruins, dragon

# Danger at Dunwater

A coastal town is alarmed when a nearby lizardfolk colony starts buying weapons and armor in secret. Your class is sent to scout the colony, learn what the lizardfolk are planning, and prevent a misunderstanding from turning into war. This is a mission where diplomacy, patience, and cultural curiosity may matter more than winning fights.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 3

**Tags:** diplomacy mission, coastal mystery, faction choices

# Salvage Operation

A wealthy merchant hires your class to recover a locked strongbox from a famous ship that vanished years ago and has now been spotted drifting at sea. The job sounds simple: board the wreck, find the box, and get out. But the derelict ship is rotting, infested, and not as abandoned as it looks.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** haunted ship, salvage mission, high-stakes escape

# Reach for the Stars

Your class is sent to recover a stolen book of star lore and planar magic from the remote mansion of a brilliant but troubled scholar. The mansion has become warped by something from beyond the known planes, and the mission may turn from a simple recovery job into a rescue before an otherworldly ritual is completed.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 3

**Tags:** haunted mansion, cosmic horror, stolen book, rescue mission

# The Murkmire Malevolence

Your class attends a special exhibition at a museum of natural history, where a strange archaeological discovery is about to be unveiled. But one disgraced academic believes the prized "stone" is something far more dangerous, and if she is right, the class may need to pull off an urgent after-hours recovery before the museum's newest attraction becomes a disaster.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 1

**Tags:** museum heist, eldritch threat, academic mystery, ticking clock

# Hunt for Mage Tower

Your class returns for a new school year just as Strixhaven announces the Battle of Strixhaven, a famous Mage Tower tournament where student teams compete for campus glory. As you train, choose a mascot, survive exams, and navigate campus life, strange magical incidents begin disrupting the school. Something is stalking the tournament, and game day may be more dangerous than anyone expects.

**Length:** Medium (2 levels)

**Intended Level Range:** Levels 4-6

**Tags:** school tournament, campus mystery, magical sports, student life

# Baker's Doesn't

A cozy village field trip takes a sudden turn when a confectionery catches fire and the clues point toward a missing young dragon. The search leads from a suspicious local bakery into the woods, where a candy cottage and an old bargain hide a much stranger story than anyone in town expected.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 3

**Tags:** fairy-tale horror, village mystery, hag, dragon rescue

# The Will of Orcus

Your class visits a remote mountain village near an old temple once used by a cult of Orcus. When undead and masked cultists begin stealing supplies from the locals, the investigation leads into the temple itself, where the cult's strange behavior may point to an unexpected ally.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** temple dungeon, undead cult, dragon ally, mountain village

# Fated Flight of the Recluse

Your class boards an elemental airship for what should be a routine long-distance flight, only for sky pirates, a missing captain, and dangerous secret cargo to turn the trip into a crisis in the clouds. Keeping the ship aloft may be only the first problem, especially once a dragon decides the airship should never reach its destination.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 3

**Tags:** airship crisis, sky pirates, moral dilemma, dragon encounter

# Hold Back the Dead

Your class arrives at a newly reclaimed frontier fortress expecting routine guard duty, only to find an undead horde already marching through the fog. With little support and a strange siege weapon at your assigned wall section, you'll have to hold the battlements, stop saboteurs inside the keep, and break the enemy commander before the fortress falls.

**Length:** One-shot

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** fortress defense, undead horde, siege battle, Red Wizards

# Scions of Elemental Evil

Your class is sent to investigate renewed cult activity at a ruined temple once infamous for elemental evil. Disguised as new initiates, you'll need to navigate rival cult factions, identify their leaders, and stop a ritual that could unleash an elemental army from a hidden chamber beneath the temple.

**Length:** One-shot

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** cult infiltration, elemental evil, ruined temple, planar ritual

# Uni and the Hunt for the Lost Horn

Your class follows a cry for help into a magical woodland, where a unicorn has been captured and robbed of her horn by an evil spellcaster. To save her, you'll need to cross through a portal into a strange crystal demiplane, recover the stolen horn, and escape before the villain's ritual destroys it forever.

**Length:** One-shot

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** unicorn rescue, woodland magic, demiplane, nostalgic fantasy

# X Marks the Spot

A planar field trip to the jungle world of Ixalan goes disastrously wrong when your class is captured by vampire conquistadors. After a daring escape, you're pulled into a race through dangerous wilderness to find an ancient relic before a ruthless vampire commander can claim it.

**Length:** Short (1 level)

**Intended Level Range:** Level 4

**Tags:** prison escape, jungle expedition, vampire politics, relic hunt
