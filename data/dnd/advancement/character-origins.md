This chapter describes the two components of an adventurer's origin: [[[advancement:backgrounds|background]]] and [[[advancement:races|race]]]. Together, these elements suggest your character's early experience and family origin before taking up the adventuring life and learning the capabilities of a [[[advancement:classes|class]]].

If you choose a background or a race from an older book, see the sidebar "[Backgrounds and Races from Older Books](/sources/dnd/phb-2024/creating-a-character#backgrounds-and-races-from-older-books)" in [[[advancement:Creating a Character]]] for how to use them with the options here.
