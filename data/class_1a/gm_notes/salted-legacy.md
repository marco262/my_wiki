## Session dashboard

### Quick flow (scenes)

1. **Arrive at the Dyn Singh Night Market** - establish vibes, let players browse.
2. **Caught in the Middle (Onion Chase)** → Gammon vs Annaliese public scuffle; PCs can intervene.
3. **Family Feud (Hiring Pitch)** → Kusa + Annaliese try to hire PCs to "prove" rival sabotage.
4. **Around the Market (Kasem approaches)** - offers tour; tries to steer PCs away from politics/games.
5. **Market Investigations** - check Kaputt mit Noodles/Xungoon stalls; find persimmon peels; vendors clam up.
6. **Learning More** - Market Games -> renown -> vendors share gossip.
7. **Revealing the Plot** - suspect Kasem -> investigate his tent / reveal wynlings.
8. **Conclusion** → Kasem confession → market guards → reconcile Kusa/Annaliese → reward.

### Session reminders (track this)

* **Each PC renown** starts at **0** (track separately).
* PCs level up after the group **participates in all three games**.
* If they skip games, level up when they **reveal Kasem**.
* If the party stalls out: use **Rivals Explode** (public Kusa/Annaliese scrap → spot Kasem with persimmon).

### Key checks and DCs (one-glance)

* **Break up Gammon vs Annaliese**: DC 12 Athletics *or* DC 12 Persuasion
* **Kasem's Tour (he's deflecting you)**: DC 14 Insight
* **Inspect Kaputt mit Noodles/Xungoon stall (5+ min)**: DC 14 Investigation -> persimmon peels; follow-up: "no one sells persimmons in the market"
* **Get a vendor to say anything early**: DC 10 Persuasion -> "Market business isn't for outsiders..."
* **Slip into Spicy Brothers private tent**: DC 12 Stealth
* **Kasem is hiding something**: DC 10 Insight
* **Befriend a wynling after it appears**: DC 10 Persuasion (Sylvan helps interrogate)
* **Force confession (if you have only 2/3 rumor hits)**: DC 12 Intimidation or Persuasion
* **Rivals Explode fallback**:
  * DC 12 Insight to notice Kasem watching + persimmon vanishing
  * DC 14 Intimidation to make him confess

## Encounters (initiative reminders)

### Onion Chase / scuffle

* Not really combat; use the DC 12 checks above.
* If PCs do nothing, Kusa arrives anyway and the hiring pitch happens.

### Market Games

**Greet the Heat (Aroon Family Pepper Challenge)**

* 3 rounds; each turn: roll d10 spicy pepper -> Con save DC varies
* Fail: disadvantage next save; fail by 10+: reaches for milk/stands up -> out
* Winner: +1 renown
* Keep the Random Spicy Pepper table handy (DCs 10/12/16/18/20)

**Battle Prawn Challenge**

* Goal: within **6 rounds**, (1) prep beans and (2) slay 1 prawn
* Start: "3...2...1...start!" -> **roll initiative** (PCs + prawns)
* Beans: object AC 10; hp 15; immune cold/poison/psychic; avoid acid/fire/lightning/necrotic/radiant (ruins)
* Prawns: use **Giant Sea Horse** stat block; half cover vs attacks from outside tank; stop if someone drops to 0
* Renown: +1 if success in <=6 rounds; otherwise 0

**Hide-and-Seek Challenge (Tut-krogh)**

* Goal: return **4 caterpillars in 1 minute** (= 10 rounds)
* Start: when last PC down ladder -> "Ready? Go!" -> **roll initiative**
* Find: action + DC 13 Investigation/Perception to tell if a room has a caterpillar
* Collect: DC 10 Animal Handling or DC 12 Sleight of Hand; if missed/attacked -> flees to adjacent room
* Tut-krogh use **Lizard** stat block (but "don't harm them")
* Winner: +1 renown

## Investigation spine (what solves the mystery)

### What you can find early

* **Persimmon peels** at either stall (DC 14 Investigation; 5 minutes)
  * Follow-up: **no persimmons sold in the market** (key contradiction)
* Early vendors won't talk (DC 10 Persuasion just gets "outsiders" fence)

### How to point at Kasem

* He tries to **steer PCs away** from politics/games (DC 14 Insight on tour).
* Renown gossip points to:
  * Vi leaving -> Kasem desperate
  * buying a business -> motive
  * orange/persimmon stashing + "blue monkey" -> wynlings

### Hard evidence options

1. **Sneak into Spicy Brothers supply tent** (DC 12 Stealth)
   * Find: business records; cots; crate marked "peppers" with **3 small straw-lined cages**; **box of persimmons**.
2. **Reveal a wynling**
   * Detect Evil and Good can ping fey near sabotaged stall
   * Hold out a **persimmon** at Kaputt mit Noodles/Xungoon -> wynling shows to grab it
   * Befriend: DC 10 Persuasion
   * Wynling tells (Sylvan): Kasem brought them, they live in the Spicy Brothers tent, persimmon-paid errands, not malicious.

### Confrontation options

* If you have **all three rumor hits**, Kasem confesses.
* If you have **two rumor hits**: DC 12 Intimidation/Persuasion -> confession.
* If PCs go violent: Kasem uses **Noble** stats + calls **3 wynlings** to assist.

## Renown and gossip ladder

### Renown quick rules

* **Win a Market Game**: +1 renown.
* **Lose all three games**: still +1 renown total ("tenacity").
* Vendors open up based on renown (0-3). At **3**, multiple sources point at Kasem.

### Vendor gossip ladder (fast play)

**Vi Aroon (Spicy Brothers)**

* Renown 0: had a fight with Kasem (no details)
* Renown 1: Vi is getting married + moving away
* Renown 2: Kasem is angry; told Vi leaving is a mistake
* Renown 3: Kasem delayed a supply run; returned with extra crates kept in private tent

**Madam Kulp**

* Renown 0: blunt comment about PCs
* Renown 1: tut-krogh get nervous near Kaputt mit Noodles/Xungoon stalls
* Renown 2: believes Kusa/Annaliese wouldn’t intentionally harm each other (parents’ feud trauma)
* Renown 3: saw Kasem feeding oranges to a "weird blue monkey"

**Sid Squid**

* Renown 0: changes subject -> adventuring story
* Renown 1: some vendors *like* the feud; peace strains certain deals
* Renown 2: overheard Kasem talking about buying a family business (taboo)
* Renown 3: saw Kasem stash something orange under Xungoon stall pre-sabotage

**Other vendors (generic)**

* Renown 0: redirect to their goods
* Renown 1: disturbances cause commotion
* Renown 2: "haunted" stalls; people trip over nothing
* Renown 3: saw a **blue monkey** near Kaputt mit Noodles/Xungoon tent

## Wynling Mischief (GM pacing tool)

Roll **after each Market Game** or whenever PCs are away from Kaputt mit Noodles/Xungoon stalls:

1. **Fire** at Xungoon stall -> right leg collapses; stall crooked
2. **Vanilla buns missing** from Vada's -> powdered sugar trail -> Kaputt mit Noodles
3. **Persimmon barrage** at Kaputt mit Noodles or Xungoon -> juice everywhere
4. **Wok evidence planted** at Xungoon -> DC 14 Investigation notices gnawed handle (child-sized teeth)

Use these as:

* **time pressure** (things worsen if PCs delay)
* **clue drops** (persimmon = fey bait + Kasem connection)
