## Technical Notes for All Images

**Resolution:** Aim for at least 1920x1080 or 2048x2048 for flexibility
**Style consistency:** D&D 5e official art style, Pathfinder 2e aesthetic, or high-quality fantasy digital painting
**Color palette:** Warm golds, oranges, and reds for market lighting; use blues and purples for magical elements
**Lighting:** Night market atmosphere with lanterns, torches, and magical light sources

## Recommended AI Tools

- **DALL-E 3** (via ChatGPT Plus): Best for following complex prompts
- **Midjourney v6**: Excellent for fantasy art style, use `--style raw` for more literal interpretation
- **Stable Diffusion XL** (local or via Civitai): Free, good with fantasy art models like "Dreamshaper" or "Juggernaut"

## Batch Generation Tip

You can generate these in themed batches:
1. **Scene setters** (arrival, contrast, crowd)
2. **Investigation clues** (peels, sugar trail, damaged stall)
3. **Dramatic reveals** (tent interior, wynling, confession)
4. **Resolution moments** (reconciliation, escort, celebration)
