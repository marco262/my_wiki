# The Murkmire Malevolence

## Quick Reference

* **Source:** [The Murkmire Malevolence](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,the%20murkmire%20malevolence), from *Keys from the Golden Vault*
* **Adventure Type:** Field trip, museum heist, occult containment
* **Intended Level Range:** Published for level 1
* **Party Level:** Four level 4 students plus Miss Elleewick
* **Expected Length:** Short to Medium, probably 2-4 sessions depending on heist planning
* **Tone:** Public museum wonder turning into late-night danger
* **Premise:** The class visits the Varkenbluff Museum of Natural History on the night before a major exhibit opens. The famous Murkmire Stone is actually an eldritch egg due to hatch at midnight.
* **Main Stakes:** Recover and contain the Stone before it hatches, while avoiding panic, public scandal, and the museum's security.
* **Failure Consequence:** The Stone hatches into an eldritch horror that starts feeding in the museum and grows rapidly.
* **Field Trip Hook:** The class is already on site for a special museum preview. Dr. Cassee Dannell quietly begs them for help after official adults dismiss her.

## Session Dashboard

### One-Sentence Pitch

A respectable school museum field trip becomes a secret race against the clock when the students learn the star exhibit is a monster egg that will hatch before any outside help can arrive.

### Why the Students Act

* **Time pressure:** The Stone starts pulsing at 10:30 p.m. and hatches at midnight. The school is a full day away by normal travel.
* **Teleportation limits:** The school has access to teleportation circles, but the nearest usable circle is not casually available at the museum. Getting a message to the right adult, locating a mage who can cast *teleportation circle*, and authorizing the circle use would take longer than the remaining window.
* **Security risk:** Teleportation circles are powerful but dangerous infrastructure. Museum officials do not publish circle sigils, and Varkenbluff institutions are not going to open emergency teleport access on the word of a disgraced academic.
* **Secrecy:** If the museum leadership learns Dannell has recruited the class, Alda can have them removed, the egg moved, or the story buried. A public alarm could also trap civilians in the building with the Stone.
* **They are not unsupervised:** Miss Elleewick is present. The adventure can frame this as an ugly but legitimate field judgment: the students are capable, already inside, and the clock makes them the best available option.

### Run This As

1. **Arrival and wonder:** Show the museum as a big cosmopolitan place with dinosaurs, ancient cultures, donors, and school-field-trip energy.
2. **Quiet warning:** Dr. Dannell pulls Miss Elleewick and the students aside. She has proof, but no institution will listen.
3. **Gala reconnaissance:** The class attends the private preview, meets staff, notices security, and studies the Stone.
4. **After-hours heist:** They hide inside or reenter, bypass alarms, evade guards, and swap the Stone with the jade fake.
5. **Countdown complication:** Starting at 10:30, the Stone pulses every 10 minutes and causes weird effects.
6. **Containment handoff:** Get the Stone to Dannell's crystal box. Then decide whether the school takes custody.

## Adventure Flow

1. **Field Trip Arrival**
   * The class arrives after a day's travel to Varkenbluff for a private museum preview.
   * Let them enjoy a few exhibits before the hook lands.
   * Useful visuals: [Museum Exterior](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/museum-exterior.png), [Class Field Trip](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/class-field-trip.png), [Grand Entrance](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/grand-entrance.png)
   * Music: [Museum Crowd]($load|ambience|Restaurant Walla Track-SoundBible.com-479263734.ogg)

2. **Dannell's Warning**
   * Dr. Cassee Dannell approaches privately, preferably after recognizing Miss Elleewick as a school representative.
   * She explains that the Murkmire Stone is an egg and will hatch at midnight.
   * She has a crystal box, but it is not ready to travel through the museum; she needs the Stone brought to her.
   * Useful visual: [Dr. Cassee Dannell](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/dr-cassee-dannell.png)

3. **Gala Reconnaissance**
   * The Gemstone Wing gala runs from 6 p.m. until museum close at 8 p.m.
   * The class can study the Stone, meet Alda, notice her clutch, gather gossip, and identify security measures.
   * Key clue: the pedestal will lock the room if the Stone is simply lifted away.
   * Useful visuals: [Gemstone Wing Gala](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/gemstone-wing-gala.png), [Alda Arkin](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/alda-arkin.png), [Maryam Bikram](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/maryam-bikram.png)
   * Music: [Gala Murmur]($load|ambience|crown_murmur_male.mp3)

4. **Security Planning**
   * Strong plans usually involve one or more of these:
     * steal or copy Alda's clutch contents;
     * get spare pass cards from V7;
     * find alarm records in V6;
     * locate the fake jade Stone in V9;
     * use secret passages, attic, basement, or the V12/V13 vent.
   * If the party stalls, have Dannell pass a panicked note: "The egg is warming early. Do not let them lock it away with the guests still nearby."

5. **After-Hours Heist**
   * Once the museum closes, guards patrol, alarms activate, and animated defenses come online.
   * This should feel like stealth, problem-solving, and consequences, not a fair-room combat crawl.
   * Useful visuals: [After-Hours Hallway](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/after-hours-hallway.png), [Prehistoric Predators](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/prehistoric-predators.png), [Curator's Office](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/curators-office.png)
   * Music: [Low Drones]($load|ambience|Industrial_Ambient_Drones_06.mp3)

6. **Countdown and Extraction**
   * At 10:30 p.m., start the Murkmire Stone pulses every 10 minutes.
   * The simplest clean win: swap the Stone with the V9 fake, leave through a planned route, and reach Dannell before midnight.
   * If things go loud, use guard response and the pulse effects to force movement instead of bogging down.
   * Useful visuals: [Stone Pulse](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/stone-pulse.png), [Basement Exit](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/basement-exit.png)
   * Sound: [Heartbeat Loop]($load|effect|heart_beat_loop.ogg)

7. **Containment and Aftermath**
   * Dannell seals the Stone in the crystal box; the pulses stop.
   * Recommended campaign change: Dannell asks Miss Elleewick whether the school can secure the crystal box in a warded vault until her reputation is restored.
   * This gives Headmaster David a powerful occult object to "safeguard," fitting the long-term secret arc.

## Scene Notes

### Scene: Arrival in Varkenbluff

**Purpose:** Establish distance from school, museum spectacle, and why the class is already in the right place.  
**Source Link:** [The Opening Gala](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,the%20opening%20gala)  
**Visual Aid:** [Museum Exterior](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/museum-exterior.png), [Class Field Trip](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/class-field-trip.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Museum Crowd]($load|ambience|Restaurant Walla Track-SoundBible.com-479263734.ogg)

**Setup:** The class has traveled for the day to Varkenbluff. The field trip is safe on paper: museum exhibits, a private preview, and a chaperoned educational event.

**Player Goals:**

* Explore the museum.
* Establish what each student is excited or bored by.
* Make Miss Elleewick feel like the responsible adult already present.

**Useful Framing Detail:** The trip did not use a teleportation circle. Circle sigils are controlled, expensive, and treated as security-sensitive infrastructure. The school can teleport to trusted circles, but that does not mean every museum, university, or city institution is open to emergency visitors.

**Exit Paths:**

* Dr. Dannell intercepts the class.
* Maryam corrals formal guests toward the Gemstone Wing.
* Alda gives a polished curator speech about the Stone.

### Scene: Dr. Dannell's Warning

**Purpose:** Turn the field trip into an urgent containment problem.  
**Source Link:** [A Cry for Help](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,a%20cry%20for%20help)  
**Visual Aid:** [Dr. Cassee Dannell](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/dr-cassee-dannell.png), [Murkmire Stone Close-Up](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/murkmire-stone-closeup.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Creeping Threat]($load|music|arr/BGM_Event_Ascian.mp3)

**Setup:** Dannell is a disgraced anthropologist, exhausted and scared. She has already tried university administrators, museum officials, and a direct theft. Those failures are why she is desperate enough to ask a school group.

**NPCs Present:**

* [Dr. Cassee Dannell](#dr-cassee-dannell)
* [Miss Elleewick](/class_1a/class-1a)

**Information and Clues:**

* The Murkmire Stone is an eldritch egg, not a relic.
* It will hatch at midnight.
* It cannot be damaged or destroyed.
* A prepared crystal box can neutralize it, but the box is fragile, still being sealed, and cannot be carried around the museum.
* Publicly raising the alarm risks Alda blocking access, moving the Stone, or trapping civilians inside a cover-up.

**Mechanics:**

* Dannell provides a rough museum sketch.
* If asked for proof, she can show occult notes, matching diagrams, and the first signs that the Stone is warming.
* A DC 13 Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion check confirms her evidence is more coherent than museum officials claimed.
* Fail-forward: even on a failed check, the Stone pulse later proves she was right.

**If Things Stall:** Dannell stresses that school adults cannot arrive before midnight. The choice is not "children versus qualified adults"; it is "the people already here versus no one in time."

### Scene: The Gemstone Wing Gala

**Purpose:** Social recon, security clues, and public pressure.  
**Source Link:** [Attending the Gala](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,attending%20the%20gala)  
**Visual Aid:** [Gemstone Wing Gala](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/gemstone-wing-gala.png), [Murkmire Stone Display](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/murkmire-stone-display.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Gala Murmur]($load|ambience|crown_murmur_male.mp3)

**Setup:** Formal dress, donors, gemstone cabinets, a marble pedestal, and the unsettling green Stone at the center.

**NPCs Present:**

* [Maryam Bikram](#maryam-bikram)
* [Alda Arkin](#alda-arkin)
* Donors and gala guests

**Player Goals:**

* Study the Stone and pedestal.
* Get gossip.
* Notice Alda's clutch.
* Determine how to enter or stay after hours.

**Information and Clues:**

* DC 12 Persuasion with gala guests reveals gossip:
  * Alda keeps fussing with her clutch.
  * Valuable gemstone materials are stored in the basement.
  * The museum is financially strained.
  * Alda keeps a large vintage doll in her office.
* Studying the pedestal for 1 minute reveals a defense mechanism.
* DC 12 Arcana on the pedestal confirms removing the Stone will lock every door into the room.

**Mechanics:**

* Suspicious or restricted behavior brings a guard.
* A DC 10 Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion check can send a guard elsewhere if backup has not been called.
* Sleight of Hand DC 14 can steal a pass card or key from a guard; Alda's clutch is riskier and should probably require distraction plus a check.

**Exit Paths:**

* Hide in the museum before closing.
* Leave and reenter via front doors, attic skylight, or basement doors.
* Follow Alda or target the office wing.

### Scene: Office Wing and Security Intel

**Purpose:** Give the party a fair way to solve the heist.  
**Source Link:** [Museum Locations](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,museum%20locations)  
**Visual Aid:** [Curator's Office](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/curators-office.png), [Security Records](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/security-records.png), [Marigold Doll](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/marigold-doll.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Low Drones]($load|ambience|Industrial_Ambient_Drones_06.mp3), [Squeaking Door]($load|effect|Sqeaking_door-Sarasprella-1653672487.mp3)

**Key Rooms:**

* V5 Curator's Office: patrol routes, Alda information, Marigold doll hazard.
* V6 Records Room: alarm locations, exhibit records, evidence that the Stone's occult context was suppressed.
* V7 Break Room and Storage: three spare pass cards and a potion of vitality.

**Mechanics:**

* Interior locked doors: DC 12 thieves' tools unless otherwise noted.
* Doors with active alarm spells trigger if touched without a pass card.
* Secret doors: DC 12 Perception to find from outside.
* Marigold uses the scarecrow stat block. For level 4, use it as a jump scare and alarm risk, not a major fight.

**Fail-Forward:** If they never get the office clues, let the party discover the V9 fake Stone and V12 vent by exploration. They can still solve the heist with improvisation.

### Scene: Exhibit-Level Heist Route

**Purpose:** Let the party move through the museum as a stealth puzzle.  
**Source Link:** [Varkenbluff Museum](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,varkenbluff%20museum%20of%20natural%20history)  
**Visual Aid:** [After-Hours Hallway](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/after-hours-hallway.png), [Prehistoric Predators](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/prehistoric-predators.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Low Drones]($load|ambience|Industrial_Ambient_Drones_06.mp3)

**Best Route to Surface:**

* V9 has a jade fake similar in size and weight to the Murkmire Stone.
* V12 has a vent to V13.
* V13 has the Stone and a secret passage south toward V9/V10.
* V16 basement has a secret tunnel exit to trees near the museum.

**Notable Obstacles:**

* Guards: use procedure, whistles, and backup.
* Alarms: pass cards bypass them.
* Animated statues: avoid by staying more than 5 feet away.
* Allosaurus animatronic: can be turned into a 10-minute distraction with DC 10 Arcana using thieves' tools or tinker's tools.
* Mimic in the basement: optional dangerous complication.

**Level 4 Adjustment:** Keep guards mostly normal. The danger is not that each guard can kill a student; the danger is alarms, numbers, detention, and losing the clock. Upgrade only named/security-centerpiece threats if the table wants combat.

### Scene: The Stone Swap

**Purpose:** The core heist action.  
**Source Link:** [V13: Gemstone Wing](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,v13%3a%20gemstone%20wing)  
**Visual Aid:** [Murkmire Stone Display](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/murkmire-stone-display.png), [Stone Pulse](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/stone-pulse.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Heartbeat Loop]($load|effect|heart_beat_loop.ogg)

**Setup:** Two guards patrol V13 after hours. The pedestal is fixed to the floor and protected by transmutation magic.

**Mechanics:**

* DC 12 Arcana identifies the door-locking trap.
* If the real Stone is removed without a fake, all V13 doors close and lock.
* Locked V13 doors: DC 20 thieves' tools or DC 20 Athletics to force.
* A suitable fake is in V9.
* Swapping fake for real without triggering the trap: DC 10 Sleight of Hand.
* At 10:30 p.m. and every 10 minutes after, creatures within 20 feet make a DC 10 Wisdom save against a random Murkmire Stone effect. A creature holding the Stone has disadvantage.

**Fail-Forward:**

* If the room locks, the vent to V12 and the secret door should remain possible routes after checks, magic, tools, or pressure.
* If guards arrive, Maryam tries to detain first unless the Stone's visible horror proves something is very wrong.
* If the Stone pulses in public view, let that crack the museum's confidence in Alda's story.

### Scene: Escape and Containment

**Purpose:** Resolve the clock and hand the object into campaign continuity.  
**Source Link:** [Conclusion](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,conclusion)  
**Visual Aid:** [Basement Exit](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/basement-exit.png), [Crystal Box Containment](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/crystal-box-containment.png), [School Vault Handoff](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/school-vault-handoff.png)  
**Music / Ambience:** [Creeping Threat]($load|music|arr/BGM_Event_Ascian.mp3)

**Setup:** Dannell waits nearby with the crystal box. Once sealed, the egg becomes inert and the pulse effects end.

**Outcomes:**

* Clean success: Dannell is vindicated privately; the museum avoids disaster.
* Messy success: the city watch, donors, or museum officials see enough to complicate the story.
* School custody: Miss Elleewick can recommend the Stone be sent to Professor David's school for warded safekeeping.

**Headmaster Thread:** Headmaster David later praises the students' judgment, frames the Stone as too dangerous for local politics, and places it in the school's protected collection. This should feel responsible now and suspicious only much later.

## NPCs

### Dr. Cassee Dannell

**Role:** Discredited anthropologist, quest giver, containment expert  
**Appears In:** Dannell's warning, containment aftermath  
**Wants:** The Stone safely sealed before midnight and her warnings taken seriously.  
**Knows:** Murkmire Stone is an egg, hatches at midnight, cannot be destroyed, crystal containment works.  
**Personality:** Brilliant, anxious, direct, humiliated by institutional dismissal.  
**How They Treat Students:** With more respect than is probably comfortable; she knows asking children is unreasonable, but time has made every better option fail.  
**Useful Table Detail:** Her hands shake when she opens her notes, but her voice steadies when she explains the evidence.  
**Visual / Stat Block:** [Portrait](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/dr-cassee-dannell.png), [5e.tools stat block](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#dr.%20cassee%20dannell_kftgv)

### Alda Arkin

**Role:** Museum curator, social obstacle, likely future antagonist  
**Appears In:** Gala, office/security clues, aftermath  
**Wants:** The museum's prestige protected and the Stone display to succeed. Secretly, she is tied to illicit object fencing.  
**Knows:** Dannell believes the Stone is dangerous; she dismisses or suppresses that theory.  
**Personality:** Polished, condescending, defensive when challenged.  
**How They Treat Students:** As charming donors' children or a nuisance, depending on whether they ask hard questions.  
**Useful Table Detail:** She keeps one hand behind her back on her clutch whenever security or Dannell is mentioned.  
**Visual / Stat Block:** [Portrait](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/alda-arkin.png), [Noble](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#noble_mm)

### Maryam Bikram

**Role:** Security captain and lawful pressure  
**Appears In:** Gala entrance, guard response  
**Wants:** A safe event and no embarrassing incidents.  
**Knows:** Security procedures, guest list, and guard routes. Not initially aware the Stone is dangerous.  
**Personality:** Rigid, professional, not cruel.  
**How They Treat Students:** Like underage guests who need boundaries; more stern with Miss Elleewick if trouble starts.  
**Useful Table Detail:** She gives clear warnings before consequences. If shown hard supernatural evidence, she can become a reluctant ally.  
**Visual / Stat Block:** [Portrait](^class_1a/murkmire_malevolence/maryam-bikram.png), [Guard](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#guard_mm)

### Miss Elleewick

**Role:** PC teacher, chaperone, field judgment anchor  
**Appears In:** Entire adventure  
**Wants:** Keep the students safe, make the right call under impossible time pressure.  
**How to Use:** Let the player decide how much authority she exercises. The notes assume she can justify immediate action because she is the responsible adult on site.

## Monsters and Hazards

### Museum Guards

**Appears In:** Throughout museum  
**Stat Block:** [Guard](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#guard_mm)  
**Role in Adventure:** Patrols, witnesses, social pressure, escalating consequence.  
**Tactics / Behavior:** Detain, call backup, lock routes, protect guests. They should not open with lethal force against children unless the party does something extreme.  
**Adjustments for This Party:** Keep most as standard guards. For Maryam, consider guard captain flavor or a nonlethal veteran-style stat line only if combat becomes central.

### Animated Statues

**Appears In:** V1 and V12  
**Stat Block:** [Animated Armor](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#animated%20armor_mm)  
**Role in Adventure:** Alarm-triggering magical sentries.  
**Important Rules:** Animate if a character comes within 5 feet after hours. Avoidable if discovered.  
**Adjustments for This Party:** Use pairs, positioning, and guard arrival to make them matter. Do not rely on their raw damage.

### Marigold, Alda's Doll

**Appears In:** V5 Curator's Office  
**Stat Block:** [Scarecrow](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#scarecrow_mm)  
**Role in Adventure:** Creepy office defense and noise risk.  
**Description for Table:** A human-sized vintage doll in silk, embroidered with the name "Marigold," turning its stitched face toward the intruder.  
**Adjustments for This Party:** If it feels too weak, give it max hit points and let the alarm response be the true danger.

### Animatronic Allosaurus

**Appears In:** V12 Prehistoric Predators  
**Stat Block:** [Allosaurus](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#allosaurus_mm), changed to Construct and immune to poison, psychic, charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned  
**Role in Adventure:** Optional distraction or chaos button.  
**Important Rules:** DC 10 Arcana with tinker's tools or thieves' tools overloads it; it rampages for 10 minutes, then shuts down.  
**Adjustments for This Party:** This is a tool, not just a monster. Reward clever use.

### Basement Mimic

**Appears In:** V16 Basement  
**Stat Block:** [Mimic](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#mimic_mm)  
**Role in Adventure:** Optional basement complication.  
**Adjustments for This Party:** Keep it if the escape route feels too easy; skip it if the session is already tense or late.

### Murkmire Stone

**Appears In:** V13, extraction route  
**Rules Link:** [The Murkmire Stone](https://5e.tools/adventure.html#kftgv,1,the%20murkmire%20stone)  
**Role in Adventure:** Countdown hazard and objective.  
**Important Rules to Preload:**

* 10-pound, light-green, nearly indestructible egg.
* At 10:30 p.m., becomes translucent and pulses every 10 minutes.
* Pulse: creatures within 20 feet make DC 10 Wisdom save.
* Holder has disadvantage.
* Failed save causes one random effect from the Murkmire Stone Effects table.
* Effects end when the egg hatches or is sealed in Dannell's crystal box.

### Eldritch Horror Hatchling

**Appears In:** Failure route, optional emergency finale  
**Stat Block:** [Eldritch Horror Hatchling](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#eldritch%20horror%20hatchling_kftgv)  
**Role in Adventure:** Consequence if midnight hits.  
**Adjustments for This Party:** For level 4 PCs, the hatchling is manageable if isolated. Make the threat about civilians, feeding, growth, and unstable museum terrain rather than pure stat difficulty.

## Campaign Adaptation Notes

**School Framing:** This is a planned museum field trip that overlaps with the special gala preview for the Murkmire Stone. The class is not sent to steal anything; the dangerous part is accidental.

**Why the Students Are Involved:** They are physically present, gifted, supervised by Miss Elleewick, and able to act before midnight.

**Why Adult NPCs Do Not Solve It:** The school is a day's travel away, teleportation circle use is rare and controlled, the museum is not going to open a security-sensitive route on short notice, and official local adults already dismissed Dannell.

**Tone Adjustments:** Keep the opening bright and school-trip-like. Let the horror emerge slowly through warmth, pulsing light, intrusive whispers, and institutional denial.

**Level / Balance Adjustments:** Emphasize stealth, alarms, procedures, and time. Do not upscale every guard into a lethal combatant. If combat becomes necessary, use max HP for defensive constructs, add guard waves, or use the environment instead of inflating all stat blocks.

**Continuity Links:** The Stone is an ideal object for the headmaster to take into school custody. In the moment that looks responsible; later it can read as part of his pattern of collecting powerful objects through the students.
