# CLASS 1-A CHARACTER BIBLE

# HEIGHT ORDER

1. Meli (Tallest)
2. Gren
3. Alyse
4. Timtom
5. Miss Ellee (Shortest)

---

# GREN

Species: Goliath  
Gender: Female  
Age: 11  
Role: Student

## Core Visual Identity

A broad, sturdy preteen girl who looks like a child first and a goliath second. She should feel like a taller-than-usual pudgy preteen still growing out of her baby fat, but emotionally earnest and approachable.

## Build

- Stocky
- Broad shoulders
- Thick forearms
- Strong legs
- Sturdy stance
- Athletic without being muscular
- Never slender
- Never graceful in an elf-like way

## Face

- Round face
- Pudgy cheeks
- Large green eyes
- Round nose
- Soft childlike features
- Expressive eyebrows
- Looks young and innocent

## Hair

- Dark green hair
- Slightly messy
- Often adorned with a yellow flower

## Skin

- Gray with a green tint

## Accessories

- Wooden staff with a stone charm and feathers hanging off the end

## Common Expressions

- Awe
- Excitement
- Worry
- Determination
- Earnest curiosity

## Avoid

- Adult proportions
- Sharp cheekbones
- Angular jaw
- Glamorous appearance
- Warrior-barbarian aesthetic
- Looking older than 13

# ALYSE

Species: Drow  
Gender: Female  
Age: 12  
Role: Student

## Core Visual Identity

A skeptical and intelligent drow teenager who often looks like she's already identified three flaws in everyone's plan.

## Build

- Slim
- Graceful
- Slightly reserved posture
- Compact and agile

## Face

- Dark skin
- White hair
- Sharp features
- Intelligent, purple eyes
- Frequently unimpressed

## Hair

- White
- Well-kept
- Elegant but practical

## Skin

- Purple-gray

## Common Expressions

- Skepticism
- Suspicion
- Dry amusement
- Focused curiosity
- Wary fascination

## Common Poses

- Arms crossed
- Stroking chin
- Leaning against walls
- Watching others before acting

## Avoid

- Excessive friendliness
- Wide cheerful smiles
- Hyperactive energy

---

# MELI

Species: High Elf  
Gender: Female  
Age: 13  
Role: Student

## Core Visual Identity

The oldest and tallest student, barely a teenager. Disciplined, responsible, and protective.

## Build

- Tallest member of Class 1-A
- Athletic
- Upright posture
- Long limbs
- Confident physical presence

## Face

- Elegant elven features
- Serious expression
- Calm, blue eyes with lavender highlights 
- Controlled emotions

## Hair

- Blue hair
- Long
- Well maintained

## Skin

- Lightly-tanned peach

## Common Expressions

- Calm focus
- Protective concern
- Determination
- Professional seriousness

## Common Poses

- Standing guard
- Watching for danger
- One hand near weapon or belt
- Positioned slightly ahead of the group

## Avoid

- Goofy expressions
- Chaotic body language
- Looking timid

---

# TIMTOM

Species: Gnome  
Gender: Male  
Age: 13  
Role: Student

## Core Visual Identity

A relentlessly gregarious gnome child who wants to know everything and talk to everyone.

## Build

- Small
- Youthful
- Energetic
- Constantly in motion

## Face

- Open and expressive
- Curious, yellow eyes
- Friendly smile

## Hair

- Bright red hair

## Skin

- Orange-tinted tan

## Accessories

- Pointed hat

## Common Expressions

- Fascination
- Excitement
- Surprise
- Enthusiasm

## Common Poses

- Pointing
- Leaning in
- Talking animatedly
- Looking where he shouldn't

## Avoid

- Grim seriousness
- Stoicism
- Menacing expressions

---

# MISS ELLEE

Species: Gnome  
Gender: Female  
Age: Elderly  
Role: Teacher

## Core Visual Identity

A beloved teacher whose enthusiasm consistently overwhelms her size.

## Build

- Smallest member of the group
- Aged and stout
- Elderly but energetic
- Lively posture

## Face

- Friendly smile
- Bright eyes
- Kind features
- Age visible but not frail

## Hair

- Gray hair

## Skin

- Lightly-tanned peach

## Common Expressions

- Delight
- Encouragement
- Excitement
- Patient concern

## Common Poses

- Lecturing
- Gesturing enthusiastically
- Leading tours
- Keeping students organized

## Avoid

- Frailty
- Harshness
- Cynicism
- Villainous expressions

---

# GROUP SCALE REFERENCE

Meli is clearly the tallest.

Gren is noticeably shorter than Meli but taller than Alyse.

Alyse is shorter than Gren but substantially taller than either gnome.

Timtom reaches approximately Alyse's chest.

Miss Ellee is slightly shorter than Timtom.

When grouped together, the height differences should be immediately obvious at a glance.
