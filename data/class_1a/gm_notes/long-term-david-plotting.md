## Professor David

### Murkmire Stone Correspondence

**Adventure:** [The Murkmire Malevolence](murkmire-malevolence/The Murkmire Malevolence)  
**Surface Story:** Dr. Casey Dannell has been in intermittent correspondence with an old colleague who encouraged him to compare the Murkmire Stone to obscure fringe historical accounts.  
**Hidden Truth:** The old colleague is Professor David. His exact cover story or identity in the correspondence is still TBD.  
**David's Goal:** Steer Casey toward the right theory early enough that the students get involved and ultimately return the Murkmire Stone to the school.

**What the Players May Notice Now:**

* Casey mentions an old colleague rather than discovering the theory entirely on his own.
* Casey can produce recent letters or copied notes if pressed for proof.
* Miss Elleewick and the students are approached partly because Casey trusts Professor David's school more than the museum.
* If the Stone is safely contained, Professor David is the natural adult to suggest as the next custodian.

**Why This Works as a Breadcrumb:**

* It feels like a plausible academic contact, not a planted clue.
* It explains why Casey had just enough of the right information to be dangerous and correct.
* It creates a reusable pattern for later reveals: David quietly curates information flows around powerful objects.

**Future Reveal Options:**

* The handwriting or phrasing in the letters later matches another document tied to David.
* The "old colleague" identity turns out to be a false name, title, or intermediary used by David.
* Another scholar or adventurer mentions having received similarly well-timed guidance from a mysterious learned contact.
* A later stash of correspondence shows David steering multiple people toward artifacts, monsters, or sites he wants brought under school control.

**Open Questions / TBD:**

* What name does David use in the correspondence?
* Does Casey know the colleague personally, or only by reputation and letters?
* Is David writing directly, or through a cutout?
* How much truth is David telling in the letters versus selectively curating evidence?
