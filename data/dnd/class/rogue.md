<p>Rogues rely on cunning, stealth, and their foes’ vulnerabilities to get the upper hand in any situation. They have a knack for finding the solution to just about any problem. A few even learn magical tricks to supplement their other abilities. Many Rogues focus on stealth and deception, while others refine skills that help them in a dungeon environment, such as climbing, finding and disarming traps, and opening locks.</p>
<p>In combat, Rogues prioritize subtle strikes over brute strength. They would rather make one precise strike than wear an opponent down with a barrage of blows.</p>
<p>Some Rogues began their careers as criminals, while others used their cunning to fight crime. Whatever a Rogue’s relation to the law, no common criminal or officer of the law can match the subtle brilliance of the greatest Rogues.</p>

<h2 id="RogueClassFeatures" class="compendium-hr">Rogue Class Features</h2>
<p>As a Rogue, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified Rogue levels. These features are listed in the Rogue Features table.</p>
            
            
            <h4 id="Expertise-10292324">
                
                    Level 1:
                Expertise
            </h4>
            <p>You gain Expertise in two of your skill proficiencies of your choice.</p>
<p>At Rogue level 6, you gain Expertise in two more of your skill proficiencies of your choice.</p>
            
            <h4 id="SneakAttack-10292325">
                
                    Level 1:
                Sneak Attack
            </h4>
            <p>You know how to strike subtly and exploit a foe&rsquo;s distraction. Once per turn, you can deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you have Advantage on the roll and the attack uses a <a class="tooltip-hover weapon-properties-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/equipment#Finesse" data-tooltip-href="/weapon-properties/2-tooltip">Finesse</a> or a Ranged weapon. The extra damage&rsquo;s type is the same as the weapon&rsquo;s type.</p>
<p>You don&rsquo;t need Advantage on the attack roll if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the target, the ally doesn&rsquo;t have the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#IncapacitatedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/7-tooltip">Incapacitated</a> condition, and you don&rsquo;t have Disadvantage on the attack roll.</p>
<p>The extra damage increases as you gain Rogue levels, as shown in the Sneak Attack column of the Rogue Features table.</p>
            
            <h4 id="ThievesCant-10292326">
                
                    Level 1:
                Thieves’ Cant
            </h4>
            <p>You picked up various languages in the communities where you plied your roguish talents. You know Thieves&rsquo; Cant and one other language of your choice.</p>
            
            <h4 id="WeaponMastery-10292327">
                
                    Level 1:
                Weapon Mastery
            </h4>
            <p>Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Daggers and Shortbows.</p>
<p>Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Scimitars and Shortswords.</p>
            
            <h4 id="CunningAction-10292328">
                
                    Level 2:
                Cunning Action
            </h4>
            <p>Your quick thinking and agility allow you to move and act quickly. On your turn, you can take one of the following actions as a Bonus Action: <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#DashAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/4-tooltip">Dash</a>, <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#DisengageAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/5-tooltip">Disengage</a>, or <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#HideAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/8-tooltip">Hide</a>.</p>
            
            <h4 id="RogueSubclass-10292329">
                
                    Level 3:
                Rogue Subclass
            </h4>
            <p>You gain a Rogue subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Rogue levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass&rsquo;s features that are of your Rogue level or lower.</p>
            
            <h4 id="SteadyAim-10292330">
                
                    Level 3:
                Steady Aim
            </h4>
            <p>As a Bonus Action, you give yourself Advantage on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this feature only if you haven&rsquo;t moved during this turn, and after you use it, your Speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.</p>
            
            <h4 id="AbilityScoreImprovement-10292331">
                
                    Level 4:
                Ability Score Improvement
            </h4>
            <p>You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Rogue levels 8, 10, 12, and 16.</p>
            
            <h4 id="CunningStrike-10292332">
                
                    Level 5:
                Cunning Strike
            </h4>
            <p>You&rsquo;ve developed cunning ways to use your Sneak Attack. When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can add one of the following Cunning Strike effects. Each effect has a die cost, which is the number of Sneak Attack damage dice you must forgo to add the effect. You remove the die before rolling, and the effect occurs immediately after the attack&rsquo;s damage is dealt. For example, if you add the Poison effect, remove 1d6 from the Sneak Attack&rsquo;s damage before rolling.</p>
<p>If a Cunning Strike effect requires a saving throw, the DC equals 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus.</p>
<p><strong><em>Poison (Cost: 1d6).</em></strong> You add a toxin to your strike, forcing the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#PoisonedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/11-tooltip">Poisoned</a> condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#PoisonedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/11-tooltip">Poisoned</a> target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.</p>
<p>To use this effect, you must have a <a class="tooltip-hover item-tooltip" href="/equipment/492-poisoners-kit" data-tooltip-href="/adventuring-gear/492-tooltip">Poisoner’s Kit</a> on your person.</p>
<p><strong><em>Trip (Cost: 1d6).</em></strong> If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ProneCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/12-tooltip">Prone</a> condition.</p>
<p><strong><em>Withdraw (Cost: 1d6).</em></strong> Immediately after the attack, you move up to half your Speed without provoking <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#OpportunityAttack" data-tooltip-href="/actions/1001-tooltip">Opportunity Attacks</a>.</p>
            
            <h4 id="UncannyDodge-10292333">
                
                    Level 5:
                Uncanny Dodge
            </h4>
            <p>When an attacker that you can see hits you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to halve the attack&rsquo;s damage against you (round down).</p>
            
            <h4 id="Evasion-10292335">
                
                    Level 7:
                Evasion
            </h4>
            <p>You can nimbly dodge out of the way of certain dangers. When you&rsquo;re subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw and only half damage if you fail. You can&rsquo;t use this feature if you have the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#IncapacitatedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/7-tooltip">Incapacitated</a> condition.</p>
            
            <h4 id="ReliableTalent-10292336">
                
                    Level 7:
                Reliable Talent
            </h4>
            <p>Whenever you make an ability check that uses one of your skill or tool proficiencies, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.</p>
            
            <h4 id="ImprovedCunningStrike-10292339">
                
                    Level 11:
                Improved Cunning Strike
            </h4>
            <p>You can use up to two Cunning Strike effects when you deal Sneak Attack damage, paying the die cost for each effect.</p>
            
            <h4 id="DeviousStrikes-10292341">
                
                    Level 14:
                Devious Strikes
            </h4>
            <p>You&rsquo;ve practiced new ways to use your Sneak Attack deviously. The following effects are now among your Cunning Strike options.</p>
<p><strong><em>Daze (Cost: 2d6).</em></strong> The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or on its next turn, it can do only one of the following: move or take an action or a Bonus Action.</p>
<p><strong><em>Knock Out (Cost: 6d6).</em></strong> The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or it has the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#UnconsciousCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/15-tooltip">Unconscious</a> condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. The Unconscious target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.</p>
<p><strong><em>Obscure (Cost: 3d6).</em></strong> The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, or it has the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#BlindedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/1-tooltip">Blinded</a> condition until the end of its next turn.</p>
            
            <h4 id="SlipperyMind-10292342">
                
                    Level 15:
                Slippery Mind
            </h4>
            <p>Your cunning mind is exceptionally difficult to control. You gain proficiency in Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.</p>
            
            <h4 id="Elusive-10292344">
                
                    Level 18:
                Elusive
            </h4>
            <p>You&rsquo;re so evasive that attackers rarely gain the upper hand against you. No attack roll can have Advantage against you unless you have the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#IncapacitatedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/7-tooltip">Incapacitated</a> condition.</p>
            
            <h4 id="EpicBoon-10292345">
                
                    Level 19:
                Epic Boon
            </h4>
            <p>You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.</p>
            
            <h4 id="StrokeofLuck-10292346">
                
                    Level 20:
                Stroke of Luck
            </h4>
            <p>You have a marvelous knack for succeeding when you need to. If you fail a <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#D20Test" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/41-tooltip">D20 Test</a>, you can turn the roll into a 20.</p>
<p>Once you use this feature, you can&rsquo;t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.</p>
            <h2 id="RogueSubclasses" class="compendium-hr">Rogue Subclasses</h2>
<p>A Rogue subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Rogue levels, as specified in the subclass.</p>

<p><strong><em>Prepared Spells of Level 1+.</em></strong> You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose three level 1 Wizard spells.</p>
<p>The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Rogue levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Arcane Trickster Spellcasting table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Wizard spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Arcane Trickster Spellcasting table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you&rsquo;re a level 7 Rogue, your list of prepared spells can include five Wizard spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.</p>
<p><strong><em>Changing Your Prepared Spells.</em></strong> Whenever you gain a Rogue level, you can replace one spell on your list with another Wizard spell for which you have spell slots.</p>
<p><strong><em>Spellcasting Ability.</em></strong> Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your Wizard spells.</p>
<p><strong><em>Spellcasting Focus.</em></strong> You can use an <a class="tooltip-hover item-tooltip" href="/equipment/544-arcane-focus" data-tooltip-href="/adventuring-gear/544-tooltip">Arcane Focus</a> as a Spellcasting Focus for your Wizard spells.</p>

                    Level 3:
                Mage Hand Legerdemain
            </h4>
            <p>When you cast <a class="tooltip-hover spell-tooltip" href="/spells/2619008-mage-hand" data-tooltip-href="/spells/2619008-tooltip">Mage Hand</a>, you can cast it as a Bonus Action, and you can make the spectral hand <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#InvisibleCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/8-tooltip">Invisible</a>. You can control the hand as a Bonus Action, and through it, you can make Dexterity (<a class="tooltip-hover skill-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills" data-tooltip-href="/skills/4-tooltip">Sleight of Hand</a>) checks.</p>
            
            <h4 id="MagicalAmbush-10292913">
                
                    Level 9:
                Magical Ambush
            </h4>
            <p>If you have the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#InvisibleCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/8-tooltip">Invisible</a> condition when you cast a spell on a creature, it has <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Disadvantage" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/53-tooltip">Disadvantage</a> on any saving throw it makes against the spell on the same turn.</p>
            
            <h4 id="VersatileTrickster-10292914">
                
                    Level 13:
                Versatile Trickster
            </h4>
            <p>You gain the ability to distract targets with your <a class="tooltip-hover spell-tooltip" href="/spells/2619008-mage-hand" data-tooltip-href="/spells/2619008-tooltip">Mage Hand</a>. When you use the Trip option of your Cunning Strike on a creature, you can also use that option on another creature within 5 feet of the spectral hand.</p>
            
            <h4 id="SpellThief-10292915">
                
                    Level 17:
                Spell Thief
            </h4>
            <p>You gain the ability to magically steal the knowledge of how to cast a spell from another spellcaster.</p>
<p>Immediately after a creature casts a spell that targets you or includes you in its area of effect, you can take a Reaction to force the creature to make an Intelligence saving throw. The DC equals your spell save DC. On a failed save, you negate the spell&rsquo;s effect against you, and you steal the knowledge of the spell if it is at least level 1 and of a level you can cast (it doesn&rsquo;t need to be a Wizard spell). For the next 8 hours, you have the spell prepared. The creature can&rsquo;t cast it until the 8 hours have passed.</p>
<p>Once you steal a spell with this feature, you can&rsquo;t use this feature again until you finish a <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/82-tooltip">Long Rest</a>.</p>

    <h2 id="Assassin" data-content-slug="players-handbook" >Assassin 
</h2>
    <p><em>Practice the Grim Art of Death</em></p>
<p>An Assassin’s training focuses on using stealth, poison, and disguise to eliminate foes with deadly efficiency. While some Rogues who follow this path are hired killers, spies, or bounty hunters, the capabilities of this subclass are equally useful for adventurers facing a variety of monstrous enemies.</p>
    
            <h4 id="Assassinate-10292916">
                
                    Level 3:
                Assassinate
            </h4>
            <p>You&rsquo;re adept at ambushing a target, granting you the following benefits.</p>
<p><strong><em>Initiative.</em></strong> You have <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Advantage" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/4-tooltip">Advantage</a> on Initiative rolls.</p>
<p><strong><em>Surprising Strikes.</em></strong> During the first round of each combat, you have <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Advantage" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/4-tooltip">Advantage</a> on attack rolls against any creature that hasn&rsquo;t taken a turn. If your Sneak Attack hits any target during that round, the target takes extra damage of the weapon&rsquo;s type equal to your Rogue level.</p>
            
            <h4 id="Assassin’sTools-10292917">
                
                    Level 3:
                Assassin’s Tools
            </h4>
            <p>You gain a <a class="tooltip-hover item-tooltip" href="/equipment/542-disguise-kit" data-tooltip-href="/adventuring-gear/542-tooltip">Disguise Kit</a> and a <a class="tooltip-hover item-tooltip" href="/equipment/492-poisoners-kit" data-tooltip-href="/adventuring-gear/492-tooltip">Poisoner’s Kit</a>, and you have proficiency with them.</p>
            
            <h4 id="InfiltrationExpertise-10292918">
                
                    Level 9:
                Infiltration Expertise
            </h4>
            <p>You are expert at the following techniques that aid your infiltrations.</p>
<p><strong><em>Masterful Mimicry.</em></strong> You can unerringly mimic another person&rsquo;s speech, handwriting, or both if you have spent at least 1 hour studying them.</p>
<p><strong><em>Roving Aim.</em></strong> Your Speed isn&rsquo;t reduced to 0 by using Steady Aim.</p>
            
            <h4 id="EnvenomWeapons-10292919">
                
                    Level 13:
                Envenom Weapons
            </h4>
            <p>When you use the Poison option of your Cunning Strike, the target also takes 2d6 Poison damage whenever it fails the saving throw. This damage ignores Resistance to Poison damage.</p>
            
            <h4 id="DeathStrike-10292920">
                
                    Level 17:
                Death Strike
            </h4>
            <p>When you hit with your Sneak Attack on the first round of a combat, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus), or the attack&rsquo;s damage is doubled against the target.</p>

<p>Any features in this subclass that use a Psionic Energy Die use only the dice from this subclass. Some of your powers expend a Psionic Energy Die, as specified in a power&rsquo;s description, and you can&rsquo;t use a power if it requires you to use a die when your Psionic Energy Dice are all expended.</p>
<p>You regain one of your expended Psionic Energy Dice when you finish a <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ShortRest" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/101-tooltip">Short Rest</a>, and you regain all of them when you finish a <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/82-tooltip">Long Rest</a>.</p>
<p><strong><em>Psi-Bolstered Knack.</em></strong> If you fail an ability check using a skill or tool with which you have proficiency, you can roll one Psionic Energy Die and add the number rolled to the check, potentially turning failure into success. The die is expended only if the roll then succeeds.</p>
<p><strong><em>Psychic Whispers.</em></strong> You can establish telepathic communication between yourself and others. As a <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#MagicAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/2-tooltip">Magic</a> action, choose one or more creatures you can see, up to a number of creatures equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and then roll one Psionic Energy Die. For a number of hours equal to the number rolled, the chosen creatures can speak telepathically with you, and you can speak telepathically with them. To send or receive a message (no action required), you and the other creature must be within 1 mile of each other. A creature can end the telepathic connection at any time (no action required).</p>
<p>The first time you use this power after each <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/82-tooltip">Long Rest</a>, you don&rsquo;t expend the Psionic Energy Die. All other times you use the power, you expend the die.</p>

                    Level 3:
                Psychic Blades
            </h4>
            <p>You can manifest shimmering blades of psychic energy. Whenever you take the <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#AttackAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/1-tooltip">Attack</a> action or make an <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#OpportunityAttack" data-tooltip-href="/actions/1001-tooltip">Opportunity Attack</a>, you can manifest a Psychic Blade in your free hand and make the attack with that blade. The magic blade has the following traits:</p>
<div class="spell-components">

            <h4 id="SoulBlades-10292923">
                
                    Level 9:
                Soul Blades
            </h4>
            <p>You can now use the following powers with your Psychic Blades.</p>
<p><strong><em>Homing Strikes.</em></strong> If you make an attack roll with your Psychic Blade and miss the target, you can roll one Psionic Energy Die and add the number rolled to the attack roll. If this causes the attack to hit, the die is expended.</p>
<p><strong><em>Psychic Teleportation.</em></strong> As a Bonus Action, you manifest a Psychic Blade, expend one Psionic Energy Die and roll it, and throw the blade at an unoccupied space you can see up to a number of feet away equal to 10 times the number rolled. You then teleport to that space, and the blade vanishes.</p>
            
            <h4 id="PsychicVeil-10292924">
                
                    Level 13:
                Psychic Veil
            </h4>
            <p>You can weave a veil of psychic static to mask yourself. As a <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#MagicAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/2-tooltip">Magic</a> action, you gain the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#InvisibleCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/8-tooltip">Invisible</a> condition for 1 hour or until you dismiss this effect (no action required). This invisibility ends early immediately after you deal damage to a creature or you force a creature to make a saving throw.</p>
<p>Once you use this feature, you can&rsquo;t do so again until you finish a <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/82-tooltip">Long Rest</a> unless you expend a Psionic Energy Die (no action required) to restore your use of it.</p>
            
            <h4 id="RendMind-10292925">
                
                    Level 17:
                Rend Mind
            </h4>
            <p>You can sweep your Psychic Blades through a creature&rsquo;s mind. When you use your Psychic Blades to deal Sneak Attack damage to a creature, you can force that target to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus). If the save fails, the target has the <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#StunnedCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/14-tooltip">Stunned</a> condition for 1 minute. The Stunned target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.</p>
<p>Once you use this feature, you can&rsquo;t do so again until you finish a <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/82-tooltip">Long Rest</a> unless you expend three Psionic Energy Dice (no action required) to restore your use of it.</p>

    <h2 id="Thief" data-content-slug="players-handbook" >Thief 
</h2>
    <p><em>Hunt for Treasure as a Classic Adventurer</em></p>
<p>A mix of burglar, treasure hunter, and explorer, you are the epitome of an adventurer. In addition to improving your agility and stealth, you gain abilities useful for delving into ruins and getting maximum benefit from the magic items you find there.</p>
    
            <h4 id="FastHands-10292926">
                
                    Level 3:
                Fast Hands
            </h4>
            <p>As a Bonus Action, you can do one of the following.</p>
<p><strong><em>Sleight of Hand.</em></strong> Make a Dexterity (<a class="tooltip-hover skill-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills" data-tooltip-href="/skills/4-tooltip">Sleight of Hand</a>) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with <a class="tooltip-hover item-tooltip" href="/equipment/495-thieves-tools" data-tooltip-href="/adventuring-gear/495-tooltip">Thieves’ Tools</a> or to pick a pocket.</p>
<p><strong><em>Use an Object.</em></strong> Take the <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#UtilizeAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/11-tooltip">Utilize</a> action, or take the <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#MagicAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/2-tooltip">Magic</a> action to use a magic item that requires that action.</p>
            
            <h4 id="Second-StoryWork-10292927">
                
                    Level 3:
                Second-Story Work
            </h4>
            <p>You&rsquo;ve trained to get into especially hard-to-reach places, granting you these benefits.</p>
<p><strong><em>Climber.</em></strong> You gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.</p>
<p><strong><em>Jumper.</em></strong> You can determine your jump distance using your Dexterity rather than your Strength.</p>
            
            <h4 id="SupremeSneak-10292928">
                
                    Level 9:
                Supreme Sneak
            </h4>
            <p>You gain the following Cunning Strike option.</p>
<p><strong><em>Stealth Attack (Cost: 1d6).</em></strong> If you have the <a class="tooltip-hover action-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#HideAction" data-tooltip-href="/actions/8-tooltip">Hide</a> action&rsquo;s <a class="tooltip-hover condition-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#InvisibleCondition" data-tooltip-href="/conditions/8-tooltip">Invisible</a> condition, this attack doesn&rsquo;t end that condition on you if you end the turn behind <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Cover" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/32-tooltip">Three-Quarters Cover</a> or <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Cover" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/33-tooltip">Total Cover</a>.</p>
            
            <h4 id="UseMagicDevice-10292929">
                
                    Level 13:
                Use Magic Device
            </h4>
            <p>You&rsquo;ve learned how to maximize use of magic items, granting you the following benefits.</p>
<p><strong><em>Attunement.</em></strong> You can attune to up to four magic items at once.</p>
<p><strong><em>Charges.</em></strong> Whenever you use a magic item property that expends charges, roll 1d6. On a roll of 6, you use the property without expending the charges.</p>
<p><strong><em>Scrolls.</em></strong> You can use any <a class="tooltip-hover magic-item-tooltip" href="/magic-items/9229085-spell-scroll" data-tooltip-href="/magic-items/9229085-tooltip">Spell Scroll</a>, using Intelligence as your spellcasting ability for the spell. If the spell is a cantrip or a level 1 spell, you can cast it reliably. If the scroll contains a higher-level spell, you must first succeed on an Intelligence (<a class="tooltip-hover skill-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills" data-tooltip-href="/skills/6-tooltip">Arcana</a>) check (DC 10 plus the spell&rsquo;s level). On a successful check, you cast the spell from the scroll. On a failed check, the scroll disintegrates.</p>
            
            <h4 id="Thief’sReflexes-10292930">
                
                    Level 17:
                Thief’s Reflexes
            </h4>
            <p>You are adept at laying ambushes and quickly escaping danger. You can take two turns during the first round of any combat. You take your first turn at your normal <a class="tooltip-hover rule-tooltip" href="/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Initiative" data-tooltip-href="/rules-glossary/76-tooltip">Initiative</a> and your second turn at your Initiative minus 10.</p>
